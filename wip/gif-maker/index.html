The checkerboard is the transparent background.

Subtitles are set below the picture in Space Grotesk, the devine.be typeface, on a solid band: GIF transparency is 1-bit (a pixel is either fully visible or fully invisible), so text drawn straight onto transparency would lose its smooth edges. The picture itself keeps its transparency, which also means soft edges and shadows get a hard cutoff — images with crisp edges work best. A GIF is a fixed grid of pixels: on a Retina / HiDPI screen an 800 px GIF shown at 800 px is stretched 2×, so export at twice the display size.
